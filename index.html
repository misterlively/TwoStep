📖 Game Rules & Testing

Goal: Solve as many two-step equations (ax + b = c) as possible before time expires.

  • 100 Seeded Problems: Every player receives the exact same 100 equations in the same order.
  • Scratchpad Work Area: Use your mouse, touch, or stylus to work out intermediate steps directly below the equation.
  • Bottom Answer Entry: Enter the variable's integer value at the bottom of the screen and press Enter.
  • Host Control: The host sets match time and can click "End Game Now" at any second to stop the match.
  • Podium & Review: At game end, the host screen shows a celebration podium with dancing avatars for the top 3, while players can step through their attempted equations one at a time with full algebraic work steps!
